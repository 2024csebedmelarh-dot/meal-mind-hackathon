The Problem We Saw

Every day, kitchens face the same question: “How much food should we prepare tomorrow?” Meal Mind turns everyday data into a practical answer.

🍚

Food Waste

Preparing too much food can create avoidable leftovers and unnecessary waste.

👥

Changing Attendance

Student attendance changes from day to day, making fixed cooking quantities unreliable.

📊

No Simple Prediction

Kitchen teams need an easy way to learn from previous attendance and leftover patterns.

Our Innovation: Meal Mind

A simple data-driven concept for smarter meal planning in colleges, hostels and community kitchens.

01 · Collect

Record daily attendance and the quantity of food left after meals.

02 · Learn

Use historical patterns to understand how attendance relates to food consumption.

03 · Predict

Estimate the next day's cooking quantity before the kitchen starts preparing food.

04 · Improve

Compare predictions with actual leftovers and continuously improve future planning.
